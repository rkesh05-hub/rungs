    How to play

    1. Type a word close in meaning to the last word on your ladder. If they link, it joins the ladder.
    2. Two words link when one is among the other's 50 nearest words, out of 30,000 English words.
    3. Reach the target in five guesses. A word that doesn't link uses a guess. Unknown words and repeats are free.
    4. Every ladder can be done in two: one middle word, then the target. Fewer guesses wins, time breaks ties.
    5. Tap an earlier word to step back to it. A hint shows a bridge word's first letter and uses a guess.

    wine → grape → fruit → banana

    The bar beside each word shows how close it is to the target. Closeness comes from word vectors, which place words used in similar sentences near each other. A new ladder appears every day at midnight.
